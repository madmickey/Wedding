FAQs

Kids?

Yes, children are more than welcome. Our two will be there. We’ll add any kid-friendly timing notes closer to the date.

Dress code?

Smart, relaxed wedding attire. Think seaside celebration, not black tie.

Parking?

There is no parking at the venue. If you do drive, there is free parking only in up in North Bondi on side streets, or paid at the beach parking, but it is expensive. If it say 2P it means thats for 2 hours. If it says 2P meter, you pay (max 2 hours). If nothing, its free.
