Local tips

Where we are

We live in Bondi in our tiny 2 bed flat 5/22a New St. It's basically halfway between Bondi Junction (a Westfield mall with everything, Coles, Woolworths etc, and train links to everywhere) and Bondi beach down where the venue is (which has Woolworths, Harris farm, and busses all day (and night)). We tend to walk day or night (20min), but busses are frequent and easy (tap on and off with your credit card). Roughly, the coast line is N-S. East is the ocean and the snazzy burbs, 6kms in is the city, then the inner west(boho), followed by the WEST, and then the Blue mountains. Bondi beach roughly aligns to this too.

Accomodation

Airbnb of course, along with Meriton serviced apartments in Bondi junction. Anyway further west than the city is a bit far probably.. down towards Mascot/airport is cheaper.

Hungry and thirsty?

If you're in Bondi, our favourite day eats are Lox stock and barrel, Blackwood, Harry's, the depot, with the Bondi public school hosting market Sat/ Sun. Afternoon drinks with a view best at North Bondi RSL, or Icebergs RSL (in the south), or just at the Pavilion(center). Night favourites are on Hall st the local bikini bar, The beekeeper, or mammasaaan for a cocktail l, beach road for a beer, or even icebergs restaurant for a posh cocktail (note RSL and restaurant are seperate).

Touristing?

Well, if you're near Bondi, you've got Icebergs salt water swimming pool, the coastal walk to Coogee, then a bus ride away (or a convaluted ferry ride!) Art Gallery NSW (Aboriginal and excellent underground tank exhibit), the Botanical gardens, and Opera house all around the same spot. Not far is the Rocks, MCA (contemporary art), the harbour bridge (you can climb it for a price). Darling Harbour with IMAX, amazing water play for kids, Chinese gardens, and China town are just in the city too. Near there is also the aquarium (it's got a Dugong!), and a ferry ride over to Taronga zoo!
